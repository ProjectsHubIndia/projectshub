1 Introduction

Thank you for purchasing projects, tools, and services from ProjectsHub. We strive to provide high-quality deliverables and AI-powered solutions. This Refund Policy outlines the limited conditions under which we offer refunds for our products.

2 Eligibility for Refunds

Our policy generally does not allow for refunds or cancellations. However, there is one exception:

✅

If you completed payment but were never granted access to the agreed deliverable or service, you may be eligible for a refund. Please contact us within 7 days of your purchase date.

3 Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be granted in the following cases:

🚫 You simply don't like the deliverable or it doesn't meet personal expectations.
🚫 You have already downloaded, accessed, or reviewed any portion of the delivered content.
🚫 The issue arises from your own device, browser, or internet connection.
🚫 A price change occurred after your purchase — pricing may change at any time without prior notice.
⚠️

Service transfers or switches to a different project/plan are not permitted. Our team's decision regarding such requests is final and binding.

4 Final Decision

All refund decisions made by ProjectsHub are final. We reserve the right to deny any refund request that does not meet the strict criteria outlined in this policy. We appreciate your understanding and encourage you to reach out before purchasing if you have any questions.

5 Changes to This Refund Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date. Please review this page periodically to stay informed about our policies.
